=====================================
EdgeLocalist — User Guide
=====================================

Everything you need to know to get the most out of EdgeLocalist.


========== STANDALONE VERSION ==========

1. CREATE YOUR ACCOUNT
----------------------
Visit edgelocalist.app and click "Start Free Trial" or "Log In." You can sign up with your email and a password, or use the Magic Link option to receive a one-click login link via email.

After signing in for the first time, you'll be asked to enter your 8-digit Rep Number (your Cutco representative ID). This connects your contacts to your account and is required before you can access the dashboard.


2. UPLOAD YOUR CONTACTS
------------------------
Navigate to the Contacts tab on your dashboard. Click "Choose File" or drag and drop a CSV file containing your customer data.

Required columns: First Name, Last Name, Address, City, State, Zip, Phone

Optional columns: Business Name, Category, Lifetime Value, Last Order Date, Gift Count, Order Count, Relationship

The system automatically detects common column name variations (e.g., "First" or "FirstName" both work). After upload, addresses are geocoded in batches — you'll see a progress indicator showing how many contacts have been processed.


3. SEARCH FOR NEARBY CONTACTS
------------------------------
Go to the Search tab. Type any address, city, or ZIP code into the search bar. Select your search radius — 5, 10, 25, or 50 miles — and click Search.

Results appear as a scrollable list sorted by distance, and as color-coded pins on an interactive map. Pin colors indicate lifetime value: green for higher-value customers, yellow for mid-range, and red for lower-value.


4. VIEW CONTACT DETAILS
------------------------
Click any contact in the results list or tap a pin on the map to open the Contact Detail card. This shows the contact's full address, distance from your search center, lifetime value, last order date, and Cutco-specific metrics like gift count and order count.


5. QUICK ACTIONS
----------------
Each contact card includes one-tap action buttons:

  - Call — opens your phone dialer with the contact's number
  - Text — opens your messaging app with a pre-filled introduction
  - Directions — opens Google Maps with turn-by-turn directions to the contact's address


6. ACCOUNT SETTINGS
-------------------
Click Settings in the top navigation to manage your account. You can update your display name, view your rep number and subscription plan, and access billing management.


7. DATA & SECURITY
------------------
Your contacts are stored securely with encryption at rest and in transit. Row-level security ensures that only you can see your own data — no other representative has access to your contacts. You can delete all your contacts at any time from the Contacts tab.


========== EDGE DMS INTEGRATION VERSION ==========

1. GETTING STARTED
------------------
EdgeLocalist is installed directly inside your Edge DMS account by your administrator. Once installed, you'll find it in your CRM's app menu. Simply click to open — no separate account or password is needed. The system automatically provisions your account using a secure single sign-on connection.


2. CONTACT SYNC
---------------
When you first open EdgeLocalist, it automatically syncs all your CRM contacts. This includes fetching contact details, saving them securely, and geocoding addresses so they're searchable by location.

The sync progress dialog shows each step in real-time:

  1. Connecting to Edge DMS
  2. Fetching your contacts
  3. Saving to your account
  4. Geocoding addresses
  5. Preparing search results

After the initial sync, contacts refresh automatically every 24 hours. Real-time webhook updates keep individual contact changes in sync instantly. You can also trigger a manual sync anytime with the Refresh button on your control panel.


3. NEARBY SEARCH
----------------
Enter any address, city, or ZIP code into the search bar on the Search tab. Choose your radius — 5, 10, 25, or 50 miles — and hit Search.

Results are displayed as a sortable list and as color-coded pins on an interactive map. Use the map legend to understand pin colors, which reflect each contact's lifetime value.


4. CONTACT DETAILS & ACTIONS
-----------------------------
Click any contact to open their detail card. You'll see their full address, distance, revenue metrics, last order date, and Cutco-specific data.

Quick action buttons let you:

  - Call — dial the contact directly
  - Text — send a pre-filled SMS
  - Directions — get Google Maps directions
  - LC Call — initiate a call through your Edge DMS calling system (appears automatically in Edge DMS mode)


5. GROUP SMS
------------
After running a nearby search, click "Group SMS" to send a personalized text message to multiple contacts at once.

Choose from pre-built templates (appointment scheduling, follow-ups, holiday greetings) or write your own custom message. Messages are personalized with each contact's first name and city using smart merge fields.

A live preview shows exactly what your contacts will receive before you send.


6. TAG CONTACTS
---------------
Select contacts from your search results and click "Tag Contacts" to apply a custom tag. The tag syncs back to your Edge DMS CRM, making it easy to organize follow-up lists and build targeted campaign audiences.

Tags are applied in batches for reliability, and you'll see a confirmation showing how many contacts were successfully tagged.


7. SECURITY & PRIVACY
---------------------
All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. OAuth tokens are managed server-side and automatically refreshed — they're never exposed to your browser. Row-level security policies ensure complete data isolation between representatives.

Webhook updates from your CRM are verified before processing. SMS sending is rate-limited to prevent abuse.
